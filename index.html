How BioPay Works

Three simple steps to a completely phone-free payment experience

01

Register & Link Bank

Create your BioPay account and securely link your bank account. Your identity is verified through government-grade KYC.

02

Enroll Fingerprint

Visit any BioPay-enabled terminal or enrollment center to register your fingerprint biometric template securely.

03

Pay at Any Vendor

Walk up to any BioPay vendor, place your finger on their scanner, confirm the amount on screen — payment done in 0.3 seconds!

01

Register Business

Sign up your business on BioPay. Provide your GSTIN, bank account details, and business verification documents.

02

Get BioPay Terminal

Receive your BioPay fingerprint scanner terminal. Set it up at your counter — it connects via USB or Bluetooth to any display device.

03

Accept Payments Instantly

Enter the amount on your device, customer scans their finger, and funds are transferred to your account in real-time.

About BioPay

The Future of Phone-Free Payments

BioPay transforms everyday retail transactions by replacing phones, cards, and wallets with your unique biometric fingerprint.

Our Vision & Mission

In a world tethered to smartphones, drained batteries, spotty mobile network connections, and forgotten wallets, BioPay was founded to create true financial freedom.

By linking a customer's verified bank account with bank-tokenized biometric templates under strict RBI and NPCI standards, customers can walk into any store, touch a 500 DPI optical sensor, and pay in less than 0.3 seconds — without touching a phone.
